Quad Clash support

A party game for 1 to 4 players on one phone.

Something wrong, or an idea? Email info@faran.io. Tell us your phone model and what happened, and we'll get back to you.

How do I play?

Lay the phone flat on a table. Each player taps their own corner of the screen to join; computer players fill the empty seats. Tap PLAY for a tournament of minigames, or FREE PLAY to pick one. Each corner has that player's buttons, so everyone plays at once.

How does a tournament work?

Every minigame pays orbs: 6 for 1st, 4 for 2nd, 2 for 3rd, and ties share. After the last game, the player with the most orbs wins the tournament. Games sometimes come with a twist, like a turbo treadmill or two bombs at once.

How do I unlock more characters?

The orbs your human players win go into your crew's orb bank. New characters join the crew at 60 and 150 orbs. Pick yours by tapping your corner on the title screen.

Why does it ask my age?

So that players under 13 only ever get ads that are safe for children: not personalized, rated for general audiences, with no advertising ID sent. The answer stays on your device.

How do I remove the ads?

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase in the game's menu, behind a grown-ups' question. If you reinstall or get a new phone, use Restore Purchase in the same place.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device. There are no accounts, so progress doesn't move between phones, except through your phone's own iCloud or Google backup if it's turned on. Deleting the app deletes your progress.
