Quad Clash support

Privacy policy

Quad Clash. Last updated: October 10, 2026.

Quad Clash is a game made by Faran, LLC.

No accounts, no analytics of our own. Quad Clash has no sign-in, no chat and no way to share anything with other players. We don't run analytics or tracking of our own, and we never see who you are.

Your progress stays on your device. Your crew orbs, unlocked characters, best scores, settings, the answer to the age question and whether you bought Remove Ads are stored on your phone or tablet, in the app's own storage. If you have device backups turned on, your phone may include them in your own iCloud or Google backup, which we can't see. Deleting the app deletes them.

The age question. The first time the game starts, it asks how old the player is. The answer only decides which ads may be shown (see below) and stays on your device.

Ads. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob between games, never during play and never when the game starts. Players 13 and over can also choose to watch an ad for bonus orbs. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address and how you interact with ads, as described in how Google uses information from apps that use its services. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, players 13 and over are asked for consent through Google's consent message, and can change their choice from the game's sound and privacy menu.

Children. Quad Clash is made for kids and grown-ups alike. For players under 13, and before the age question is answered, every ad request is marked as directed to children: the advertising ID is not sent, ads are never personalized or based on interests, there is no remarketing, and only ads rated for general audiences are shown. Children never see the "watch an ad" offers, and the Remove Ads purchase sits behind a grown-ups' question.

Purchases. Remove Ads is a one-time purchase made through Google Play or the App Store. Google or Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details. The game only learns that the purchase was made, so it can stop showing ads.

Changes. If a future version changes any of this, we will update this page and the app's store listing before that version ships.
