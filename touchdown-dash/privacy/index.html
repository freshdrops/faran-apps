Touchdown Dash support

Privacy policy

Touchdown Dash. Last updated: October 9, 2026.

Touchdown Dash is a game made by Faran, LLC.

We collect no data. Touchdown Dash has no accounts, no ads, no purchases, no analytics and no tracking. The game makes no network requests: it plays fully offline.

Your progress stays on your device. Your stars, unlocked players, best scores, settings and Daily Drive results are stored on your phone or tablet, in the app's own storage. If you have device backups turned on, your phone may include them in your own iCloud or Google backup, which we can't see. Deleting the app deletes them.

Sharing. When a grown-up taps SHARE and answers the grown-ups' question, the game makes a video of the drive or a picture of the season card on your device and opens your system's share sheet. It goes only where you send it. The game never records your camera, your microphone or anything outside the game.

Children. Touchdown Dash is made for kids and grown-ups alike. It collects nothing from anyone, children included, shows no ads and has nothing to buy. Anything that leaves the game (the share sheet) sits behind a grown-ups' question.

Changes. If a future version changes any of this, we will update this page and the app's store listing before that version ships.
