How do I play?

Before each play, pick RUN or PASS. Swipe up or down (or tap the arrows) to cut between lanes, tap JUKE to dart past a tackler, and SPIN to bounce off one. Gain 10 yards in four downs for a first down, and score a touchdown to win the game.

What are play cards?

After every win you pick one of three cards, like an extra juke, a stiff arm or a second chance. They last the whole season, so every season plays a little differently.

How do I unlock more players?

Each win earns 1 to 3 stars for its style: beat tacklers in a row, make close calls, break long runs. Every 3 stars unlocks the next player, and each player has a trait such as speed or an extra juke.

It's too hard (or too easy)

EASY is the default: moves go on one tap, and you get one free second chance each season. HARD puts every juke, spin and throw on a timing meter. Pick it on the pick-your-player screen when you start a season.

What's the Daily Drive?

A new challenge every day: one rival and three play cards, the same for everyone. Play it as often as you like. Your first touchdown of the day earns stars.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device. There are no accounts, so progress doesn't move between phones, except through your phone's own iCloud or Google backup if it's turned on. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

Does it need the internet, or cost anything?

No. Touchdown Dash plays fully offline, with no ads, no purchases, no accounts and no tracking.
