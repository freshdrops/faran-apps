OVERDUB support

A rhythm shooter where every take you record joins the band.

Something wrong, or an idea? Email info@faran.io. Tell us your phone model and what happened, and we'll get back to you.

How do I play?

Slide anywhere on the screen to move. Your instrument fires on the beat, and where you stand decides what you hit and which notes you play. When a take ends, it plays again as a ghost beside you while you record the next one on another instrument. Clear a song by stopping every slab before it breaks through the waveform line.

What happens in a boss battle?

The boss strikes the stage on the beat. The lanes it's about to hit turn red and count down. Step out of them: a player caught in a strike is knocked out for a bar and plays nothing until it wears off. Your ghosts replay their takes exactly, so a ghost that dodged when you recorded it dodges again.

There's no sound on my iPhone

Turn the volume up. OVERDUB plays its music even with the silent switch on.

How do I start a song over?

On the screen between takes, tap START OVER.

Where are my saved videos?

SAVE VIDEO opens your phone's share sheet. Choose Save Video to keep it in your photos, or send it wherever you like.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device. There are no accounts, so progress doesn't move between phones, except through your phone's own iCloud or Google backup if it's turned on. Deleting the app deletes your progress. To start over on purpose, use SETUP, then ERASE PROGRESS.

Does it need the internet?

No. OVERDUB plays fully offline, with no ads, no accounts and no tracking.
