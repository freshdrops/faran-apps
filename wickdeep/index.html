Wickdeep support

A tiny candle knight versus the dark.

Something wrong, or an idea? Email info@faran.io. Tell us your phone model and what happened, and we'll get back to you.

I bought something and don't see it

Open Wax Shop from the title screen and tap Restore Purchases. Relics and the Starter Pack's heroes come back on a new phone or after a reinstall. Wax and gold are spent as you play, so they don't come back.

Refunds

Payments go through the App Store or Google Play, so refunds do too: Apple's Report a Problem or Google Play refunds.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device. There are no accounts, so progress doesn't move between phones, except through your phone's own iCloud or Google backup if it's turned on. Deleting the app deletes your progress. To start over on purpose, use Settings, then Reset all progress.

Does it need the internet?

No. Wickdeep plays fully offline, with no ads and no tracking. Buying something needs a connection to the store.

Privacy

Wickdeep collects no data. Read the privacy policy.
