Gridluck support

A block puzzle roguelite with Pip, a lucky pixel ladybug.

Something wrong, or an idea? Email info@faran.io. Tell us your phone model and what happened, and we'll get back to you.

I bought the full game and don't see it

Open Settings from the title screen and tap Restore purchase. The full game comes back on a new phone or after a reinstall, on the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with.

What is free?

Classic, the Daily (all 8 stages), the weekly challenge and every run up to the stage 2 boss. One purchase unlocks the full game for good: all 8 stages in every run, endless mode and full seeded runs.

Refunds

Payments go through the App Store or Google Play, so refunds do too: Apple's Report a Problem or Google Play refunds.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device. There are no accounts, so progress doesn't move between phones. Deleting the app deletes your progress. To start over on purpose, use Settings, then Reset all progress; that keeps the full game.

Does it need the internet?

No. Gridluck plays fully offline, with no ads and no tracking. Buying the full game needs a connection to the store.

Privacy

Gridluck collects no data. Read the privacy policy.
