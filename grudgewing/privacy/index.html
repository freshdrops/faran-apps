Grudgewing support

Privacy policy

Grudgewing. Last updated: October 10, 2026.

Grudgewing is a game made by Faran, LLC.

We collect no data. Grudgewing has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The game itself makes no network requests.

Purchases. Optional in-app purchases go through Apple's App Store or Google Play, which handle payment under their own privacy policies. We never see your payment details. On Android, Google's billing library, which the game uses to sell through Google Play, also sends Google its own diagnostic data.

Your progress stays on your device. Settings, scales, unlocks and saved runs are stored on your phone or tablet, in the app's own storage. If you have device backups turned on, your phone may include them in your own iCloud or Google backup, which we can't see. Deleting the app deletes them. You can also erase them in Settings > Reset all progress.

Children. Grudgewing collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Changes. If a future version changes any of this, we will update this page and the app's store listing before that version ships.
